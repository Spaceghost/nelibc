TinyCC decimal digit-append profile and pointer optimization
============================================================

Profile
-------
The exact decimal parser appends digits beyond the 19/20-digit significand to
`exact_integer` by multiplying its BigUInt by 10 and adding one digit. The
TinyCC diagnostic counted 473,200 append calls and 15,631,600 limb iterations
per parser benchmark on the 600-digit corpus (800 parser calls), or about
591 append calls and 19,539 limb iterations per parse. The short corpus made
123,200 append calls and 431,200 limb iterations over 28,000 parser calls.
The long decimal append work is much larger than the 306,800 Knuth limb
iterations counted across all three parsers in the same benchmark.

Change and shootout
-------------------
Added a TCC-only rolling-pointer loop for decimal BigUInt digit appends. Other
compilers keep the indexed loop. The arithmetic and digit order are unchanged.

Twenty-four alternating TinyCC/glibc pairs show short parser gains of 0.7%,
2.0%, and 1.0% for `strtof`, `strtod`, and `strtold`; long parser gains are
14.5%, 13.5%, and 13.4%. The candidate passed the TinyCC 1,250-case float
differential corpus. Zig/glibc, GCC/glibc, and Zig/musl source builds also
passed the float differential and Knuth quotient-precheck matrices. Standalone
TinyCC smoke passed. The TCC-specific implementation is retained.

Raw profile counts are in `2026-10-22-float-decimal-append-counts-short.csv`
and `2026-10-22-float-decimal-append-counts-long.csv`. Paired shootout data is
in `2026-10-22-float-decimal-append-pointer-ab.csv`; final compiler/libc
matrices are in `2026-10-22-float-decimal-pointer-final-short.csv` and
`2026-10-22-float-decimal-pointer-final-long.csv`.
