TinyCC Knuth 32-bit carry/borrow shootout
=========================================

Candidate
---------
Changed the TCC limb-subtraction loop to keep product carry and borrow in
`cuint`, compute the low-word difference with 32-bit wrapping arithmetic, and
detect borrow with 32-bit comparisons. The Knuth function's stack frame fell
from 0x140 to 0x130 bytes in TinyCC's generated object.

Results
-------
Twelve alternating TinyCC parser pairs, current source versus candidate:

| Workload | Parser | Current median ns/call | Candidate median ns/call | Paired median change |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Short | strtof | 615.05 | 618.68 | 0.52% slower |
| Short | strtod | 577.97 | 578.84 | 0.84% slower |
| Short | strtold | 701.76 | 709.03 | 0.44% slower |
| Long | strtof | 43,793.77 | 45,254.87 | 2.05% slower |
| Long | strtod | 43,474.76 | 44,557.06 | 3.54% slower |
| Long | strtold | 44,519.18 | 45,221.28 | 1.68% slower |

Reject the candidate. The smaller frame did not compensate for the extra
instructions in the common limb loop. Production source remains unchanged.
Raw paired measurements: `2026-10-22-float-knuth-u32-carry-borrow-ab.csv`.
