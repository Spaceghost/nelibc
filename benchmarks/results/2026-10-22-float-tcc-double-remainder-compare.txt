TinyCC fixed-shift remainder comparison
Date: 2026-10-22

TinyCC's remainder-rounding disassembly showed that the generic virtual shift
comparison calls generic shifted-used and shifted-limb helpers, including
constant division/modulo helpers for the fixed shift of one bit. A specialized
compare computes the used limb count for twice the remainder and compares the
shifted limbs directly, avoiding the generic helpers.

The specialized compare passed TinyCC/glibc float differential cases. Twelve
alternating A/B pairs against the current rolling-pointer implementation
improved all short cases by 2.1-3.2% using the median paired change. Long-input
results were mixed: the median paired change ranged from 0.5% slower to 2.2%
faster, with high run-to-run variation.

The helper and call are compiled only for TinyCC. The four-way float
1,250-case matrix and quotient-precheck matrix passed after this change; the
standalone TinyCC smoke also built and ran. Final host/Nelibc short and long
shootouts are preserved in the paired artifact's final-short and final-long
CSV files.
