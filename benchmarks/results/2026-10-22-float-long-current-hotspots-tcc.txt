Current TinyCC long-parser function profile
===========================================

Instrumented the current `src/stdlib_float.nelua` in a temporary copy with
`rdtsc` timers around `big_mul_power5` and the Knuth quotient loop. The long
benchmark ran 800 parser calls per function (5 samples x 160 calls). `big_mul_power5`
ran once on every parser call; the Knuth loop ran 0.5 times per `strtof` and
`strtod` call and once per `strtold` call.

| Parser | Power5 cycles per parse | Knuth calls per parse | Knuth cycles per parse |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| strtof | 10,166.9 | 0.50 | 550.2 |
| strtod | 10,107.5 | 0.50 | 821.4 |
| strtold | 9,892.9 | 1.00 | 1,847.4 |

The power-of-five scaling path accounts for about 5.5–18.5 times as many
measured cycles per parser call as the Knuth quotient loop. The recently
measured quotient-correction loop runs zero times for `strtof` and at most once
per quotient estimate on the other long cases. That loop is not a useful
optimization target for these inputs. The persistent per-call hotspot remains
`big_mul_power5`, particularly its repeated `big_mul_small` limb work.

The timers use C `lfence; rdtsc` helpers called at the function boundaries. This
is a scoped function profile, not a complete cycle breakdown of the parser. The
profile source copy and executable are under `build/`; production source is
unchanged. Raw counts and cycle totals:
`2026-10-22-float-long-current-hotspots-tcc.csv`.
