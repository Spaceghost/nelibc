TinyCC rolling-pointer Knuth limb loop
Date: 2026-10-22

The TCC-only path keeps rolling pointers to numerator and denominator limbs
inside the multiply/subtract loop. The other compiler builds retain indexed
access. The change follows TinyCC disassembly showing repeated stack spills and
indexed-address calculations in the hot loop.

Twelve alternating baseline/candidate pairs per parser were run on short and
long inputs. TinyCC/glibc improved 2.2-2.9% on short inputs and 1.6-4.1% on
long inputs. The universal pointer candidate measured mostly flat under
Zig/glibc, GCC/glibc, and Zig/musl, with a few small regressions; therefore the
optimization is compiled only for TinyCC. Full results are in
2026-10-22-float-knuth-rolling-pointers-ab.csv.

Validation after adding the TCC-only branch:
  * make float-diff-matrix: 1,250 cases passed on Zig/glibc, GCC/glibc,
    Zig/musl, and TinyCC/glibc.
  * make float-divide-precheck-matrix: quotient-boundary checks passed on all
    four compiler/libc combinations.
  * make smoke-standalone-tcc: standalone TinyCC binary built and ran.
  * make float-shootout-matrix and make float-long-shootout-matrix completed.

Single-run final host/Nelibc matrices after the TCC-only source change are in
2026-10-22-float-knuth-rolling-pointers-final-short.csv and
2026-10-22-float-knuth-rolling-pointers-final-long.csv. These are final
implementation shootouts, not paired A/B measurements.
