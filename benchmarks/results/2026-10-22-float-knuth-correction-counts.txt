TinyCC Knuth quotient-correction counts
=======================================

Instrumented a temporary copy of `big_divide_knuth_u64` and ran the existing
short and long float parser benchmark corpora. Each short parser measurement
made 28,000 Nelibc calls; each long measurement made 800 calls. The correction
loop never ran for any of the short cases. On long inputs, it ran once per
corrected quotient estimate: 400 times in 800 `strtod` calls and 400 times in
800 `strtold` calls. It did not run in `strtof`. No estimate needed more than
one correction.

This makes the quotient-correction loop an unlikely source of substantial
parser time: it does zero work on the short corpus and averages 0.5 iterations
per long `strtod`/`strtold` call. These are instrumented counts, not timings.
The instrumentation was compiled with TinyCC and written into a temporary
source copy; production source is unchanged.

Raw counts: `2026-10-22-float-knuth-correction-counts.csv`.
