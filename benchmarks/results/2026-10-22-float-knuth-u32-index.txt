TinyCC Knuth 32-bit loop-index shootout
=======================================

Changed the Knuth limb-loop counter from `csize` to `cuint` in a temporary
Nelua source copy. Twelve alternating TinyCC parser pairs gave these medians
(current versus candidate; paired median change):

| Workload | Parser | Current ns/call | Candidate ns/call | Change |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Short | strtof | 615.29 | 623.35 | 1.20% slower |
| Short | strtod | 585.57 | 589.53 | 0.08% slower |
| Short | strtold | 711.14 | 716.20 | 0.93% slower |
| Long | strtof | 43,870.93 | 45,425.01 | 3.76% slower |
| Long | strtod | 42,822.31 | 44,853.07 | 6.60% slower |
| Long | strtold | 43,939.23 | 46,230.04 | 4.58% slower |

Reject the candidate. The loop counter width change makes long parsing slower;
production source remains unchanged. Raw paired measurements:
`2026-10-22-float-knuth-u32-index-ab.csv`.
