TinyCC hexadecimal exact-integer append profile
================================================

The regular short corpus has too few hexadecimal digits to exercise the exact
BigUInt append loop substantially. I profiled two temporary 600-digit hex
inputs: a fractional significand with `p+2400`, and an integer significand
with `p-2400`. Each parser benchmark made 800 calls.

Profile
-------
The exact hex append path performed 474,000 BigUInt append calls and
18,681,200 limb iterations per parser, or about 592 appends and 23,352 limb
iterations per parse. The distribution was identical for `strtof`, `strtod`,
and `strtold`. This is a recurring long-input cost, comparable to and slightly
larger than the decimal digit-append profile.

Change and validation
---------------------
Added a TCC-only rolling-pointer loop for hexadecimal BigUInt digit appends;
other compilers keep the indexed loop. Twenty-four alternating TinyCC/glibc
pairs show short-input timing within 0.3% of baseline and long-hex gains of
18.4–18.8% across all three parsers.

The candidate passed the 1,250-case TinyCC float differential corpus. A custom
long-hex check matched host results, end pointers, and errno for `strtof`,
`strtod`, and `strtold`. The four-way float differential and Knuth precheck
matrices passed, Zig/GCC fallbacks compiled, and standalone TinyCC smoke passed.
The TCC-specific path is retained.
