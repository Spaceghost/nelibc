TinyCC Knuth 32-bit divide shootout
===================================

Candidate
---------
A TCC/x86-64-only macro used `divl` for the normalized quotient estimate,
with a saturation case for a 64-bit quotient that reaches the 32-bit base. The
compiler generated a 32-bit `div ecx` instead of the existing 64-bit `div rcx`.
The candidate's Knuth function stack frame grew from 0x140 to 0x160 bytes,
partly because the macro wrote quotient and remainder through output pointers.

Correctness
-----------
The candidate matched the TinyCC/glibc host output on the float differential
corpus (`float-diff-host-tcc.csv` versus `float-diff-nelibc-div32.csv`).

Results
-------
Twelve alternating TinyCC parser pairs, current source versus candidate:

| Workload | Parser | Current median ns/call | Candidate median ns/call | Paired median change |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Short | strtof | 611.61 | 615.50 | 0.24% slower |
| Short | strtod | 578.83 | 578.83 | 0.40% slower |
| Short | strtold | 699.11 | 707.37 | 0.57% slower |
| Long | strtof | 43,992.51 | 45,652.75 | 2.98% slower |
| Long | strtod | 44,030.20 | 45,451.22 | 4.00% slower |
| Long | strtold | 44,141.96 | 46,171.82 | 5.97% slower |

Reject the output-pointer macro. Its wider stack frame and extra stores outweighed
the narrower divide. Production source remains unchanged. Raw paired results:
`2026-10-22-float-knuth-div32-ab.csv`.

Expression-form follow-up
------------------------
Changed the inline macro to return the quotient directly and reuse its high-word
input as the `divl` remainder output, removing the quotient/remainder output
pointers. TinyCC generated `div ecx` and reduced the frame from 0x160 to 0x150
bytes, but the parser shootout remained slower. The candidate passed the same
float differential check.

Twelve alternating parser pairs (current versus expression candidate):

| Workload | Parser | Current ns/call | Candidate ns/call | Paired median change |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Short | strtof | 613.61 | 623.26 | 1.54% slower |
| Short | strtod | 576.83 | 592.00 | 3.40% slower |
| Short | strtold | 700.54 | 721.34 | 2.90% slower |
| Long | strtof | 43,672.82 | 45,104.58 | 3.70% slower |
| Long | strtod | 43,459.97 | 45,987.93 | 6.31% slower |
| Long | strtold | 44,382.82 | 46,413.72 | 4.93% slower |

Reject the expression macro too. Raw data:
`2026-10-22-float-knuth-div32expr-ab.csv`.
